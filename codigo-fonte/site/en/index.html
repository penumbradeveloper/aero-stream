For people who want it all in one place

Everything you need to watch, play and chat with friends, in an interface made of glass and bubbles.

GPU streaming

Your graphics card does the encoding (hardware H.264) at the resolution and frame rate you choose: up to 1080p and 60 fps for free, and up to 4K and 240 fps with the subscription.

Play on a friend's PC

Remote controller, mouse and keyboard, up to 4 controllers at once. One small bubble in the corner and nothing else in front of the game.

Encrypted Aero Talk

Private and group chats with photos, voice notes and videos, plus voice and video calls. End-to-end encrypted: not even the server can read it.

Truly free

Everything works for free. Aero Bubble subscriptions are designed not to pressure anyone, while helping keep the app running.

Friends abroad

Relay servers all over the world and reconnection that survives drops: watch together from Brazil to Bosnia.

Updates itself

New version out? The app downloads it, checks the digital signature and updates. You never have to send anything to anyone again.

Only on Aero

Things you won't find in other chat apps, all free to start.

Your phone becomes a mic

No mic on your PC? Open Aero on your phone, scan the code and it becomes the call microphone over Wi-Fi.

Music together, no bot

Paste a YouTube link and the song plays at the same time for everyone in the chat or call, with a queue. No bot needed.

Watching now

A Twitch or Kick stream, a YouTube video or any site you pick shows up for your friends, with cover and link. You choose which sites.

A call that looks like you

Lights, call background colors and the color behind each person's photo, with a live preview before applying.

Create and earn from it

Build skins in the Studio and integrations for the Community. Sellers get paid straight through Mercado Pago.

Truly transparent

The source code is on GitHub for anyone to read and check the privacy. Nothing hidden.

A "console Skype", with bubbles

Aero Talk puts chats, groups and calls in one place, built to be used with a controller too.

  • Groups with an owner and admins, invite links, nicknames for friends and rules for who can join calls.
  • Profiles with a banner, colors, a bio, your Discord and a song from Spotify.
  • Emojis (with skin tones), pasted or dragged images, reply, edit and delete messages.
  • Discord Rich Presence out of the box, nothing to set up.
  • GIFs, your own stickers, mentions, pinned messages and chat search, just like Discord.
  • Read receipts (only if both people want them), two-step verification and blocking.

A one-person project

Made by one person, improved by everyone

All of Aero, from the PC app to the phone, the website and the servers, is made by one person. So there can (and will) be bugs now and then.

The app only gets better when you tell us what went wrong and what is missing. Send it through Feedback in the app's Settings: every piece of feedback is read.
