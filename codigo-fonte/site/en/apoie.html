Hi! To you who use Aero and like the app: I would like to ask for your help.

I took the time to build this app (and I keep building it) out of need, but I made it public because I am sure many of you have the same need I do: lots of people need this and get blocked by rules that make no sense.

I will not share too much, but I still live with my parents and, I know it is nobody's problem, but things at home are very hard: I live under constant pressure and go through other difficult situations. I need to fix my computer with new parts, buy a bed and, on top of that, pay for Aero's development, where I use Claude. As more people use the app, the costs grow and I will need to pay for the servers that keep accounts, messages and rooms online, for relaying calls and streams of people who cannot connect directly, for storing photos and files, for the subscription that allows more e-mails per day (like verification codes) and for the certificate that removes the security warning when installing on Windows.

Anyway, if this touches you, please donate: every cent helps, I really need it. Other than that, thank you for simply using my app. Thank you, and let us keep going strong building the environment we all deserve, an environment we need.

Subscriptions designed not to pressure anyone who uses the app, while helping keep the app running. No fear of being unique, no fear of not pleasing everyone. Peace!

— Ryan Victtor
