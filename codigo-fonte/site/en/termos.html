1. Aero and who runs it

Aero (also called Aero Stream) is an app to chat, call, watch and play together with friends, available for Windows, Linux, Android and the browser (Aero Web). It was created and is maintained independently by Ryan Victtor, with no big company behind it. In these terms, "Aero", "we" and "us" mean the project and the person who runs it; "you" means whoever uses Aero.

If you don't agree, please don't use Aero: you can sign out and ask for your account to be deleted at any time (see section 4).

5. Privacy and data

Privacy is one of Aero's foundations, and we handle your data under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

  • End-to-end encrypted chats: messages are encrypted on your device and only the people in the conversation can read them. Not even we can.
  • Only what's needed: we keep only what your account, security and payments need to work, such as name, tag, email, settings, plan and technical security logs.
  • No selling: we never sell, rent or trade your data, and Aero has no ads.
  • Payments: card and PIX details stay with Mercado Pago (or Ko-fi, for donations). Aero never sees or stores your card number.
  • Your rights: you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and withdraw any consent you gave, through Feedback in the app.
  • Security: if a security incident affects you, we will let you know as the law requires.

9. Aero Bubble (subscriptions)

Aero is free, and Aero Bubble is an optional subscription with extras (for example, cosmetics, more space and creator features). Current plans and prices are shown in the app.

  • On a card, the subscription renews monthly until you cancel. You can cancel anytime in the app, and the extras stay until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • With PIX, a payment lasts 1 month and does not renew by itself.
  • Change of mind: you have 7 days from purchase to cancel and get your money back, as guaranteed by Brazil's Consumer Protection Code. Just ask through Feedback.
  • Price changes are announced in advance and apply from the next renewal; you can cancel before that if you don't want to continue.
  • Extras have no cash value and can't be exchanged for money or moved to another account. An account banned for breaking the rules loses its extras, with no refund for the period already used, except where the law requires one.

10. Donations

Donations (through Ko-fi or PIX) are voluntary and help keep the servers and development going. They are not a purchase and, unless the app says otherwise, don't grant extras. If you donated by mistake, contact us through Feedback.

11. Store and Community creators

In the Community tab, creators can share and sell what they made: Donator subscribers and above can sell skins, and Supporter subscribers can also submit integrations.

  • How you get paid: sales are processed by Mercado Pago. Creators connect their own Mercado Pago account and receive each sale's price minus Aero's commission (currently 15%, shown before you publish) and Mercado Pago's own fees. The commission may change, always announced in advance and only for future sales.
  • Taxes and obligations: creators are responsible for the taxes and legal obligations on what they receive.
  • Chargebacks and refunds: if a sale is refunded (including the 7-day change of mind) or disputed, the matching amount may be deducted from the creator.
  • What gets published: creators guarantee the content is theirs or that they have the right to use it, that it follows sections 6 and 7 and that it works as described. Skins carry only looks (CSS and files); integrations follow the Integrations tab rules and never run hidden code.
  • Review: Aero may review, reject, pause or remove from the store any item that breaks these terms or receives justified reports, and may suspend a creator's access to the store.
  • Buyers get a personal license to use the item inside Aero, with no right to resell or redistribute it.
